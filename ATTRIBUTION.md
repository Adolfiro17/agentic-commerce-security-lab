# Attribution

## Subject source

The scenario and risk themes follow **McKinsey and Company (QuantumBlack), "The agentic commerce
opportunity: How AI agents are ushering in a new era for consumers and merchants" (October 2025)**:
https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-agentic-commerce-opportunity-how-ai-agents-are-ushering-in-a-new-era-for-consumers-and-merchants

The article is cited for its ideas (interaction models, protocols, delegated authority, know your
agent, payment and fraud risks). No text is reproduced from it.

## Format inspiration

The lab format (a deliberately vulnerable application in modules, each with a student handout,
instructor solution, deterministic tests and a findings export) draws on ideas from public AI
security training projects: taimurijlal/AI-Security-Engineering-Class, AIRT (AI Red Team Academy)
and the AI Goat projects. No code or text was copied from them.
